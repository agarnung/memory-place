Memory Place — spatial preview

A gentle reminiscence space for older adults living with mild cognitive impairment or Alzheimer's, used with a caregiver. One familiar place at a time; no scoring, no timers, no failure states.

Not a medical device. No diagnosis, treatment, recording, or identification. Use with a caregiver.
