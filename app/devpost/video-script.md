# Video script: Memory Place (target 2:30, hard limit 3:00)

Record the **Lens Studio preview** (Spectacles device, Interactive input) with a screen recorder. Move with WASD, look with right-drag, and click to pinch. Start the preview fresh (Reset) so the card begins at the front.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Title card over the preview: "Memory Place: reminiscence on Spectacles" | "Talking about familiar places and routines is one of the gentlest ways to connect with someone living with Alzheimer's. But a tablet puts a screen between two people." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Preview from the centre of the ring; slow look left to right across the six places | "Memory Place turns the room itself into the memory aid. Six familiar places stand around you: Home, Family, Music, Work, Garden and Travel." |
| 0:30–0:50 | Turn towards **Home** and click it. The card glides to sit beside the house | "Walk to a place and pinch it. One memory opens, right beside the place, so you never have to look away from it." |
| 0:50–1:10 | Hold still and let the card unfold: question, then the sensory cue after 3 s | "One open question first. Then, a moment later, a sensory cue: a wooden door and the sound of keys. There are no wrong answers; the person just talks." |
| 1:10–1:25 | Click **Repeat**: the card unfolds again | "If they need a moment, Repeat plays the memory again at the same calm pace." |
| 1:25–1:45 | Click **Caregiver**: the button lights up and the follow-up appears | "The caregiver stands beside them and sees the same card. Caregiver mode adds a follow-up question to read aloud, without taking over." |
| 1:45–2:05 | Click **Next** a few times, then finish the place (6 memories): the welcome card returns and the label says *Done* | "Next moves on when they are ready. Finishing a place never feels like failing: it is simply marked Done." |
| 2:05–2:20 | Click **Finish**: "Thank you for sharing these memories. Today you visited Home." | "Finish ends the session with a thank-you and a recap of where you went today. No score, no timer, no red error state." |
| 2:20–2:30 | Wide look across the ring; end card with "Built with Lens Studio, CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP · Not a medical device · Use with a caregiver" | "Memory Place was built for Spectacles with Lens Studio, CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP, and tested with LEAF. It is not a medical device: it is a gentle way to talk, together." |

## Before recording

- Do one practice run: the cue appears 3 s after the question, and the caregiver note 5.5 s after it. Leave those pauses in the video; they show the calm pace.
- Close the LEAF panel, or make sure no "PASSED" banner shows in the preview.
- Record at 1080p or higher; keep the preview panel large so the text is readable on YouTube.

## Rules

- Total length under 3:00.
- Clear English voice-over and English subtitles.
- No copyrighted music. Silence or a Lens Studio / royalty-free track only.
- Record the Lens Studio preview, not a desktop app.
- Show the whole flow end to end: open a place, the unfolding card, Repeat, Caregiver, Next, a finished place, Finish.
- Show that there is no score, timer or red error state.
- Do not show personal data, real photos, names or addresses.
